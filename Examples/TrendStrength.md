# TrendStrength Mode

**TrendStrength Mode**  

Interchangeable candle coloring reflects directional conviction:

🟢 Strong uptrend  
🔴 Strong downtrend  
⚪️ Neutral or indecisive  

Helps traders stay with momentum and avoid chop.
