# ⏱️ Multi-Timeframe Versatility

**Multi-Timeframe Ready**  
TrinityTrend adapts to:  
- Intraday scalping  
- Daily swing setups  
- Weekly macro bias  
- Extended macro alignment  

Toggle modes to match your strategy:

---

### 🔹 Intraday Mode
- Quick reaction for scalps and day trades  
- Short lookbacks, fast EMA/EMA, ATR(10)  
- Detects momentum bursts and reversals  
- Default squeeze settings

---

### Swing Mode
- Multi-day to multi-week setups  
- Medium lookbacks, EMA/SMA or EMA/EMA, ATR(14)  
- Filters noise, captures sustained trends  
- Default squeeze settings

---

### Macro Mode
- Position trading and long-term bias  
- Longer lookbacks, SMA/SMA, ATR(20)  
- Tracks bull/bear phases (e.g., 50/200 cross)  
- Squeeze: BB(30, 2.5), KC(30, 1.8)

---

### Extended Macro Mode
- Institutional, multi-quarter to multi-year view  
- Very long lookbacks, SMA/SMA (100/300, 200/500), ATR(50)  
- Big-picture filter for secular cycles  
- Squeeze: BB(50, 3.0), KC(50, 2.0)
